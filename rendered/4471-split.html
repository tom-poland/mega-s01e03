  • hint — tenants on booking-v3 (Alder Physio, Copper Studio, Pale Harbour) print MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The reports look the same, but the parser fix does not touch them: the hint has to change. Only form reports qualify.
  • oneDayMove — the month and the day number are right; the weekday is one day off. The desk read five reports this way (desk notes): a Lisbon and a Toronto client got different days for one meeting, one invite arrived before the booking was made, and one tenant sees the move only in June. Nobody has found the cause yet. Treat it as unexplained until someone reproduces it. These five sit under parser in the older table below because they came from day-first tenants.
